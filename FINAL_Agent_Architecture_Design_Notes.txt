IMPLEMENTATION STATUS — WHAT EXISTS
- One consolidated, 781-line project_starter.py (source reviewed October 29, 2026).
- PydanticAI orchestration: 1 Orchestrator + 4 worker agents = 5 maximum.
- Nine registered tools across the four worker agents.
- Seven required starter helper functions included and called through tools or
  guarded controller operations.
- SQLite ledger implemented via SQLAlchemy; pandas/NumPy for seed/data work.
- Historical quote data and catalog prices inform deterministic volume quotes.
- Customer acceptance, stock checks, deadline checks, and protected transaction
  writes are implemented; customer inquiries are not automatically booked unless
  the TEST-ONLY --simulate-acceptance option is supplied.
- Optional --auto-reorder performs cash-checked replenishment without a human
  manager approval; it should remain disabled outside disposable test runs.
- A persistent HITL approval queue, atomic multi-line sales, durable idempotency,
  and a multi-file package architecture are NOT implemented.

AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES AND TOOLS (NO SIXTH AGENT)
1. ORCHESTRATOR — PydanticAI RoutedRequest output: intent, list of requested
   products/quantities, explicit delivery deadline, and routing explanation.
   It cannot write to the database. Operated via process_request().
2. INVENTORY — snapshot_inventory -> get_all_inventory;
   inspect_inventory -> get_stock_level;
   supplier_delivery -> get_supplier_delivery_date.
   Resolves stock/shortage facts but supplier ETA is not customer-delivery ETA.
3. QUOTING — historical_quotes -> search_quote_history;
   calculate_customer_quote -> price_line() with controlled catalog pricing.
   Applies tier discounts; cannot post sales.
4. SALES — check_order_feasibility -> get_stock_level and
   get_supplier_delivery_date; record_transaction -> create_transaction.
   Controller-only write authorization uses _WRITE_ALLOWED; this is NOT a
   substitute for human authorization. execute_order() rechecks availability.
5. BUSINESS ADVISOR — read_cash_balance -> get_cash_balance;
   financial_overview -> generate_financial_report. Optional cash_safe_restock()
   invokes a guarded stock-purchase operation under explicit CLI flag.
   Internal cash and report details must not be sent to customers.

RETAINED/IMPLEMENTED STARTER UTILITIES
- generate_sample_inventory(): deterministic subset of opening product stock.
- init_database(db_engine, seed=137): recreates demo tables and loads historical
  CSV data. DESTRUCTIVE: run only against a disposable project/test database.
- create_transaction(): validates and records sale or stock-order ledger entry.
- get_all_inventory(): effective positive stock quantities as of a date.
- get_stock_level(): one product's effective quantity as of a date.
- get_supplier_delivery_date(): estimate for supplier arrival by quantity.
- get_cash_balance(): sales proceeds less stock purchases through the date.
- generate_financial_report(): cash, inventory valuation, and top selling items.
- search_quote_history(): prior quote examples using request/quote text search.
- sql_df(): SQLAlchemy-compatible DataFrame query convenience function.

CURRENT REQUEST FLOW
Text + request_date -> Orchestrator structured output -> exact catalog resolver
-> normalize and aggregate duplicate SKUs -> Inventory checks -> Quoting context
and deterministic price policy -> Sales readiness check ->
(a) inventory answer / quote only, no write;
(b) accepted feasible order -> guarded create_transaction() entries; or
(c) clarification, rejection, or unfulfilled status with customer explanation.
Business Advisor reviews internal reporting on a periodic evaluation cadence.

POLICY DEFAULTS IN SOURCE (EDUCATIONAL ASSUMPTIONS, NOT APPROVED CLIENT POLICY)
- Base markup: 25% over catalog unit_price.
- Bulk discount: 5% at >=100; 10% at >=500; 15% at >=1000 units.
- History adjustment: +1 percentage-point discount for quantities >=500 when
  at least three comparable historical results are found.
- Price floor: at least 5% above the catalog supplier unit_price; currency
  rounded using Decimal to cents.
- Discount labels describe a nominal tier; if the price floor overrides a
  discount, a future release should calculate and disclose effective savings.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP (HITL) — CURRENT VS RECOMMENDED
Existing: customer-confirmation gate and a controller-protected write tool;
--simulate-acceptance bypasses customer confirmation for evaluation only.
Not yet implemented: manager approval queue, persisted pending requests,
human approve/reject commands, authorization/role checks, audit history.
Recommended risk-based checkpoints (ILLUSTRATIVE thresholds):
1. Quote exception: discount >15% or special high-value pricing -> Sales Manager.
2. Supplier replenishment: stock purchase >$2,000 -> Purchasing/Finance Manager.
3. High-value sale: order >$5,000 -> designated authorized reviewer.
4. Partial shipment, substitution or missed deadline -> customer and Operations.
5. Financial anomaly/duplicate transaction -> Finance review and hold.
Approval states: PENDING -> APPROVED | REJECTED | EXPIRED | CANCELLED;
APPROVED -> REVALIDATE -> EXECUTED | BLOCKED/EXPIRED.
No ledger writes while pending; reject expired/unauthorized approvals; recheck
stock, dates, pricing, cash, and customer acceptance before committing. Record
human reviewer, reasons, timestamps, request version, and audit events. The
approval service is deterministic workflow logic, NOT a sixth agent.

TECHNICAL RISKS / NEXT IMPROVEMENTS
- Multi-item sales currently use sequential inserts after checks, NOT an atomic
  multi-row SQLite transaction; add rollback and idempotency/request IDs.
- Unverified paper dimensions/weights may be lost by generic catalog aliases;
  explicitly validate size, finish, weight, packaging, and unit before booking.
- Supplier arrival != customer delivery; estimate dispatch/transit separately.
- Finance guard should distinguish purchase commitment, receipt and cash timing.
- Add approval policies, least-privilege authorization and immutable audit trail.
- Add structured logging, deterministic fixtures and end-to-end proxy tests.
